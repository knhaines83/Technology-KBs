# Accessing the Bitwarden Website </u></b>

Go to the following website to create your free Bitwarden Password Manager account. Please note that I am creating this account using a web browser. It does not matter which browser you choose to use, such as Firefox, Safari, DuckDuckGo, or Chrome.

<p>It is easier to manage passwords in one central location than it is to manage individual account logins and passwords separately. </p>

https://vault.bitwarden.com/#/login

## Account Sign-Up Part-1:

<b> Enter the email address you want to use to log in to your Bitwarden account. </b>

- On this screen, you will need to enter your email address to create your free Bitwarden account.

- Click <b> Continue </b> to proceed with the account creation process.

#### See the example below. 

![[bitward_email_pg2.png]]

 ## Account Creation Part-2:

<p> Enter the name associated with your Bitwarden account. If this is a shared account, it does not matter whose name is used, as long as there is a name associated with the account. <p>

<p> I recommend selecting the option to receive updates from Bitwarden. </p>

<p> Then click </u> <b> Confirm </b> to continue.

#### See the example below.

![[bitward_login_pg3.png]]

### Verify Account

<p> The next screen that appears in your browser will prompt you to verify your email address. Go back to your email account and click <b> <u> Verify email </u> </b> to verify your account.

## Account Master Password Part-3:

### Create Master Password

<p> The next step is to create your <b> Master Password. </b> This password will be your primary password for logging in to your Bitwarden Vault and accessing your stored passwords. Your Master Password should be strong, unique, and something you can remember. I would also recommend having Bitwarden check for known data breaches involving your passwords, if that option is available. </p>

#### See the example below.

![[bitward_masterpass_pg5.pn]]

- Bitwarden offers browser extensions that allow you to save and update your Vault with login information, including usernames and passwords, directly from your web browsers. This is a very useful feature and is highly recommended.
